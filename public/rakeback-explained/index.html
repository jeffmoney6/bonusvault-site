What is rakeback?

Rakeback is one of the most common rewards at crypto casinos. Here's what it is, how it's calculated and how it compares with cashback and deposit bonuses.

Last checked: November 2026

Rakeback in one sentence

Rakeback gives you back a percentage of the casino's edge on every bet you place – whether you win or lose.

How rakeback is calculated

Every casino game has a house edge. If a game has a 1% edge and you wager $1,000, the casino's expected profit is about $10. With 15% rakeback, you would get roughly 15% of that, around $1.50, back. The more you wager, the more rakeback you collect.

Rakeback vs cashback

Rakeback is based on how much you bet, regardless of results. Cashback is based on your net losses over a period – if you finish in profit, there's usually nothing to pay back. Both are often paid out without wagering requirements.

Rakeback vs deposit bonus

A deposit bonus gives you a bigger balance up front but usually has wagering requirements. Rakeback is smaller per bet but often withdrawable straight away, which makes it easier to understand and use.

FAQ

Is rakeback worth it?

Rakeback reduces the cost of playing over time. It won't turn a losing session into a win, but it gives back part of the house edge on every bet.

Is rakeback paid even if I win?

Yes. Rakeback is based on how much you wager, not on whether you win or lose.

Does rakeback have wagering requirements?

Often not. Gamdom's welcome rakeback, for example, has no wagering.

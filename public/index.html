FAQ

How do I use a bonus code?

Click "Claim bonus" on the casino you want. The code is usually added automatically. If not, copy it and paste it into the referral or promo field when you register. Codes can normally only be added when you create the account.

What is rakeback?

Rakeback gives you back a percentage of the casino's edge on every bet you place, win or lose. Unlike a deposit bonus, it usually has no wagering requirement.

What does a wagering requirement mean?

It's how many times you need to bet the bonus (and sometimes your deposit) before you can withdraw it. For example, 40x on a $100 bonus means $4,000 in bets. Always read the casino's bonus terms.

Do I need to verify my identity (KYC)?

Rules differ by casino. Some ask for verification before a bonus is credited, others only on request or before larger withdrawals. See the table above.

Why can't I access a casino?

Each casino blocks countries where it isn't licensed or where online gambling is restricted. Check the "restricted countries" list in its terms. Using a VPN to get around this breaks the casinos' rules and can lead to closed accounts and lost winnings.

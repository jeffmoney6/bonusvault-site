Questions

How do I use a bonus code?

Click "Claim" on the casino you want. The code is usually added automatically. If not, copy it and paste it into the referral or promo field when you register. Codes normally can't be added after the account is created.

What is rakeback?

Rakeback gives you back a share of the casino's edge on every bet, win or lose. Unlike a deposit bonus, it usually has no wagering requirement. Try the rakeback calculator.

What does a wagering requirement mean?

It's how many times you need to bet a bonus before you can withdraw it. 40x on a $100 bonus means $4,000 in bets. Work it out with the wagering calculator.

Do I need to verify my identity?

It depends on the casino. Some verify you before the bonus is credited, others only on request or before larger withdrawals. See crypto casino KYC.

Why can't I see some casinos?

Casinos that don't accept players from your country are hidden. Using a VPN to get around this breaks the casinos' rules and can lead to closed accounts and lost winnings.

How to choose a crypto sportsbook

  • Welcome offer type: a deposit match gives more bonus money up front but comes with wagering. Rakeback and cashback give less up front but are usually withdrawable straight away.
  • Markets: if you mainly bet on esports, look for a site with a strong esports section. For mainstream sports, a bigger sportsbook usually means more markets.
  • KYC: check when verification is required – some sites ask before the bonus, others only before withdrawals.
  • Availability: every sportsbook blocks some countries. Check the restricted list in the terms before you sign up.

How rakeback is calculated

Rakeback returns a share of the casino's expected profit – the house edge – on every bet. The formula is simple: amount wagered × house edge × rakeback %. Wager $1,000 on a game with a 1% edge at 15% rakeback and you get back about $1.50.

Because rakeback depends on the house edge, games with a higher edge generate more rakeback per bet – but also cost more on average. Rakeback reduces your cost; it doesn't turn a negative expected value positive.

FAQ

How is casino rakeback calculated?

Rakeback is usually the amount wagered multiplied by the game's house edge, multiplied by your rakeback percentage.

Is 15% rakeback good?

15% is a generous rate for a welcome offer, especially without wagering. The actual amount depends on how much you play and the house edge of your games.

Do I get rakeback if I win?

Yes. Rakeback is based on how much you bet, not on whether you win or lose.

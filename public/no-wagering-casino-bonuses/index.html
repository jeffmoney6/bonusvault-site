What does "no wagering" mean?

A wagering requirement is how many times you must bet a bonus before it can be withdrawn. With 40x wagering on a $100 bonus, you need to place $4,000 in bets first. A no-wagering bonus has no such requirement: cashback or rakeback lands in your balance as real money.

No wagering vs deposit match: which is better?

A deposit match like the 200% offers at Stake and Shuffle gives you much more bonus money up front, but you'll need to meet 40x wagering before withdrawing. No-wagering rakeback and cashback give less up front, but every dollar you get back is yours immediately.

  • Choose a deposit match if you plan to play a lot anyway and want the biggest starting balance.
  • Choose no-wagering if you want simple terms and the ability to withdraw at any time.

FAQ

Are no wagering bonuses really withdrawable?

Yes. Rakeback and cashback with no wagering are credited as real balance that can be withdrawn, subject to the casino's normal withdrawal rules.

Which crypto casinos offer no wagering bonuses?

Gamdom offers 15% instant rakeback for 7 days and Roobet offers 20% cashback for 7 days, both without wagering.

Is rakeback the same as cashback?

Not exactly. Rakeback returns part of the house edge on every bet, win or lose. Cashback returns a percentage of your net losses over a period.

Why do deposit bonuses have wagering?

Wagering stops players from depositing, taking the bonus and withdrawing immediately. In return, deposit bonuses are usually larger.

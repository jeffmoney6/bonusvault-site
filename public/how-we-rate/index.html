What the score is based on

Scores are based on each casino's published terms and offers – welcome bonus, wagering rules, minimum deposit, products, verification rules and licence details. We check these against the casino's own pages and update the scores when terms change.

What the score is not: we don't currently run test deposits and withdrawals at every casino, so payout speed and customer support aren't part of the score. Always read the casino's own terms before you sign up.

Commission and rankings

BonusVault earns a commission when you sign up through our links. Rankings are ordered by the score above, using the same method for every casino.

FAQ

How are BonusVault scores calculated?

How often are scores updated?

We update scores when a casino changes its welcome offer or terms.

Welcome offer

Stake gives a 200% match on your first deposit – a much bigger starting balance – but the bonus has 40x wagering. Roobet instead returns 20% of your losses as cashback during your first 7 days, with no wagering, capped at $200 per day and $1,400 in total.

Sportsbook and casino

Both sites combine a casino and a sportsbook in one account. Stake is known for one of the biggest sportsbooks in crypto, while Roobet adds regular raffles and its RooWards rewards program.

Verdict

Pick Stake if you want the largest bonus up front and a huge sportsbook. Pick Roobet if you prefer simple, no-wagering cashback and a low $5 minimum deposit.

FAQ

Is Stake or Roobet better?

Stake offers a bigger deposit bonus and a very large sportsbook. Roobet offers no-wagering cashback and a lower minimum deposit. The better choice depends on how you play.

Which has the better welcome bonus, Stake or Roobet?

Stake's 200% match is larger but has 40x wagering. Roobet's 20% cashback is smaller but has no wagering.

Can I use both Stake and Roobet?

Yes, if both are available in your country you can open an account at each and claim both welcome offers.

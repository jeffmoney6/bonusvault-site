Responsible gambling

Gambling should always be entertainment. Here's how to stay in control, the signs that it may be becoming a problem, and where to get free, confidential help.

By the BonusVault editorial teamUpdated November 2026

Keep it fun: simple rules

  • Only gamble with money you can afford to lose.
  • Set a budget and a time limit before you start.
  • Never chase losses.
  • Don't gamble when you're stressed, upset or have been drinking.
  • Treat bonuses as entertainment value, not a way to make money.

Tools at most crypto casinos

  • Deposit limits – daily, weekly or monthly caps.
  • Loss and wager limits on some sites.
  • Cool-off periods – lock your account for a set time.
  • Self-exclusion – close your account for months or permanently.

You'll usually find these under account settings or "responsible gambling". If you can't find them, contact the casino's support.

Warning signs

  • Spending more money or time than you planned.
  • Borrowing money or selling things to gamble.
  • Hiding gambling from people close to you.
  • Feeling anxious or irritable when not gambling.
  • Gambling to win back losses or escape problems.

Where to get help

  • BeGambleAware.org – free advice and support.
  • Gamblers Anonymous – peer support groups worldwide.
  • Gambling Therapy – free online support in many languages.
Blocking software: tools such as Gamban or BetBlocker can block gambling sites on your devices.

FAQ

How do I set a deposit limit?

Most casinos offer deposit limits in account settings. Set them before you start playing.

What is self-exclusion?

Self-exclusion closes your casino account for a chosen period or permanently, so you can't gamble there.

Where can I get help with gambling problems?

Free, confidential support is available from organisations such as BeGambleAware, Gamblers Anonymous and Gambling Therapy.

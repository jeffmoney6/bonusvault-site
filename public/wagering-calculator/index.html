How wagering requirements work

A wagering requirement (also called playthrough or rollover) is the number of times you must bet a bonus before you can withdraw it. A $100 bonus with 40x wagering means $4,000 in total bets.

Some casinos apply the requirement to the bonus only. Others apply it to the deposit plus the bonus, which doubles or triples the amount you need to wager. Always check which one applies.

Example

You deposit $50 and get a 200% bonus of $100 with 40x wagering on the bonus only: 100 × 40 = $4,000 to wager. If the requirement were on deposit + bonus: (50 + 100) × 40 = $6,000.

Why the expected cost matters

Every game has a house edge. On average, the casino keeps that percentage of everything you bet. Wagering $4,000 on games with a 3% edge has an expected cost of about $120 – more than the $100 bonus. On lower-edge games, the same wagering costs less. That's why the games you play (and which games count towards wagering) matter as much as the bonus size.

FAQ

How do I calculate a wagering requirement?

Multiply the bonus amount by the wagering multiplier. If wagering applies to deposit and bonus, add them together first, then multiply.

What does 40x wagering mean?

You must bet 40 times the bonus amount (or deposit + bonus, depending on the terms) before you can withdraw it.

Do all games count towards wagering?

Not always. Many casinos count slots at 100% but table games, live casino or sports at a lower rate, or exclude some games entirely. Check the bonus terms.

Is a bonus with wagering worth it?

It can be if you plan to play anyway, but the expected cost of the wagering can exceed the bonus value on high-edge games.

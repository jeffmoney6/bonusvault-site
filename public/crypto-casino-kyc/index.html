Crypto casino KYC explained

KYC (Know Your Customer) is identity verification. Even crypto casinos can ask for it. Here's when it usually happens, what you may need, and how the casinos we compare handle it.

Last checked: November 2026

What is KYC?

KYC means proving who you are, usually with an ID document and sometimes proof of address. Licensed casinos use it to prevent fraud, underage gambling and money laundering, and to check that players aren't in restricted countries.

When do crypto casinos ask for KYC?

  • Before a bonus – some casinos verify you before crediting a welcome offer.
  • Before withdrawals – especially larger ones.
  • On request – if account activity needs to be checked.

What you might need

  • A valid ID (passport, ID card or driving licence)
  • Proof of address (e.g. a recent utility bill or bank statement)
  • Sometimes proof that you own the wallet or payment method
Important: casinos check your country during KYC. Accounts opened from restricted countries – including via VPN – can be closed and winnings withheld. Only sign up where the casino accepts players.

FAQ

Do all crypto casinos require KYC?

Most can require it at some point, even if you can start playing with basic details. Rules differ per casino.

What documents are needed for casino KYC?

Usually an ID document and sometimes proof of address or proof of wallet ownership.

Can I avoid KYC by using a VPN?

No. Using a VPN to get around restrictions breaks casino rules and can lead to closed accounts and lost winnings.

How to judge a sports betting bonus

  • Wagering multiplier: 40x on a deposit bonus means a lot of betting before you can withdraw. Check whether it applies to the bonus only or deposit plus bonus. Try our wagering calculator.
  • Minimum odds: many sports bonuses only count bets above certain odds, such as 1.30 or higher.
  • Max bet rules: some offers only count bets up to a share of your deposit.
  • Bet types: parlays, cash-outs and void bets often don't count.
  • No-wagering alternatives: rakeback and cashback give less up front but are usually withdrawable straight away.
Bet responsibly. A bonus never changes the odds in your favour. Set a budget before you start and don't chase losses.

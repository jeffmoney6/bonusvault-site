How to deposit crypto at a casino

New to crypto casinos? Here's exactly how to get crypto into your casino account – from buying your first coins to claiming your welcome bonus – without costly mistakes.

By the BonusVault editorial teamUpdated November 2026

Step-by-step

  1. Get crypto. Buy crypto on a regulated exchange where you live. USDT, Litecoin or Solana are good choices for low fees.
  2. Sign up at the casino with a promo code. Add the code during registration – most codes can't be added afterwards.
  3. Open the deposit page. Choose the coin you want to deposit.
  4. Choose the network. Pick the same network on the casino and in your exchange or wallet (for example USDT on TRON).
  5. Copy the deposit address. Use the copy button or scan the QR code – never type it by hand.
  6. Send a small test amount first if it's your first time, then send the rest.
  7. Wait for confirmation. Most networks confirm in seconds to minutes; Bitcoin can take longer.
  8. Activate your welcome offer if the casino requires it.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Sending on the wrong network.
  • Forgetting the memo/tag for coins that need one (such as XRP).
  • Depositing less than the minimum needed for the bonus.
  • Not adding the promo code before finishing registration.
Only sign up where the casino accepts players from your country. Accounts from restricted countries can be closed during verification.

FAQ

How long does a crypto casino deposit take?

Usually seconds to a few minutes on fast networks. Bitcoin can take longer depending on network load.

Is there a minimum crypto deposit?

Yes, each casino and coin has a minimum. Welcome bonuses may require a higher minimum deposit.

Do I need a wallet to deposit crypto at a casino?

You can send directly from an exchange account or from your own wallet. Make sure the network matches.

Can I deposit with a card at a crypto casino?

Some casinos offer card purchases of crypto through third-party providers. Fees are usually higher than buying on an exchange.

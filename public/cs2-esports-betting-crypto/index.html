Tips for esports betting

  • Follow the scene: roster changes, map pools and patches move odds quickly.
  • Check the format: best-of-one matches are much more unpredictable than best-of-three.
  • Compare odds across sites before placing larger bets.
  • Set a budget and stick to it – esports run almost every day, which makes it easy to overspend.
